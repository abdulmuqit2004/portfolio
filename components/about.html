About Me

My Journey

I'm a fourth-year Computer Science student at Ontario Tech University specializing in Data Science — and honestly, the thing I'm most proud of is how fast I learn. Hand me an unfamiliar tool, framework, or problem space and I'll have something working before the week is out. That's just how I'm wired.

Over the past few years I've built a lot: React apps, ML classifiers, NLP pipelines, data automation systems, and even a freelance agency. Each project taught me something new, and I deliberately chase that discomfort. I believe the gap between "I've never done this" and "I've shipped this" is just focused effort and curiosity — and I've got plenty of both.

Right now I'm deepening my understanding of machine learning, large language models, and software systems design. I don't just want to use these technologies — I want to understand them well enough to build on top of them and push them further.
